# Romania Power Map

Interactive map of power generation and storage projects in Romania, operating and in development.
Live at https://matand299.github.io/romania-power-map/

- `index.html` – the whole web app (Leaflet map, county panel, filters, pipeline, list view, CSV export)
- `data/plants.json`, `data/counties.json` – data the page loads
- `data/source/` – source spreadsheets and county boundaries
- `scripts/build_data.py` – rebuilds `data/*.json` from the master database

## Data

The map is an interim one, built from two official sources so that no plant appears twice:

- `Puteri-capacitati_24.04.2023_Final_EN_With_Status.xlsx` – the ANRE licensed capacity register (24.04.2023),
  read directly. Plants whose licence had passed its expiry date are kept and marked, since the licence may have
  been renewed. Coordinates come from the master database's copy of the same plant when it has one.
- `entsoe/` – ENTSO-E Transparency Platform, installed capacity per production unit (units of 100 MW and more),
  one file per year. `entsoe_anre_match.csv` says which register plants each unit stands for: a matched unit
  replaces those rows with its current capacity; units built after the register's date are added; capacity listed
  only for later years is shown as under construction.

- `transelectrica/` – Transelectrica (DEN) installed capacity files: `7productie23.xls` (sheet "Stocare", every storage
  installation in the grid) and `7productie12a.xls` (sheets "Fotovoltaice" and "Eolian", installed capacity per relevant solar and wind plant).
  All storage installations are added. A solar or wind plant counts as already on the map when a plant of that type
  there shares a distinctive name word or its owner at a capacity within 25%; register rows that are the same plant in
  stages or before an extension are replaced by Transelectrica's figure; otherwise the plant is added. Plants are placed at the locality their name gives;
  `transelectrica_locations.csv` settles names the other files cannot, with the source.

`Romania_Energy_Projects_Master_Database_2026_EN.xlsx` also holds Transelectrica, distribution-operator and
Global Energy Monitor rows. They are left out (`INCLUDED_SOURCES` in the build script) until each can be checked
against the register.

### Coverage against ANRE's totals

`data/anre_totals.json` holds ANRE's installed capacity by primary energy type, from its public "Puteri instalate"
report (https://anre.ro/puteri-instalate); `scripts/fetch_anre_totals.py` refreshes it. Under each technology the
"Capacity mix" panel shows the share of ANRE's figure in operation on the map and the MW between them. Each plant in
`plants.json` also carries `m23`, its capacity on the map's 2023 base (24.04.2023).

### Weekly update

`.github/workflows/weekly-data.yml` runs every Monday (or by hand from the Actions tab): it fetches ANRE's totals (`scripts/fetch_anre_totals.py`)
and Transelectrica's installed capacity files (`scripts/fetch_transelectrica.py`), rebuilds `data/*.json` with `scripts/build_data.py` and commits any change. A source that cannot be fetched keeps its
last good copy.

## Updating the data

Replace or add files in `data/source/`, then run `python3 scripts/build_data.py` and commit.

## Design

Both maps follow the Tidal HUD design system (https://claude.ai/artifact/XQajtxc2KJcTvVFvt9pgYi): dark glass panels
that fade into the map, cyan light as the highlight, Rajdhani for numbers and Barlow Semi Condensed for text. The
classic map's tokens are the CSS variables at the top of `index.html`; the 3D map's come from `assets/theme.json`.
The classic map has a night theme (the default) and a day theme, switched by the sun button among the map controls
and remembered by the browser.

## 3D map (prototype)

`map3d.html` shows the same plants as 3D models on a relief map of Romania
(https://matand299.github.io/romania-power-map/map3d.html). The classic map (`index.html`) is unchanged.

- **Style:** every colour, font, size and the starting view come from `assets/theme.json`. Edit it and reload.
- **Models:** `assets/models/<technology>.glb`, generated by `scripts/build_models.py` in a toy-like style.
  Their colours come from `models.palette` and the technology colours in the theme (the coloured rim around
  each base); after changing those, run the script again. `models.html` shows all models side by side.
  To use a designed model, replace the file with one of the same name (Y up, about 10 units across, origin at
  the centre of the base).
- **Speed:** each technology has a light model (`<name>_low.glb`) shown from afar and a detailed one shown from
  `models.detail_zoom` in; the screen resolution is capped at `map.max_pixel_ratio`; one elevation source feeds
  the terrain, the land colours and the hillshade.
- **Land:** coloured by height (`map.land`: metres → colour), with hillshade on top; neighbouring countries are faded.
- **Base map:** `data/basemap.json` (Natural Earth, `scripts/build_basemap.py`). Relief: Terrarium elevation
  tiles from AWS Open Data. Plant ground heights: `data/elevation.json` (`scripts/build_elevation.py`).
- **Libraries:** MapLibre GL 5.6.0 and deck.gl 9.1.14, stored in `assets/vendor/`, so the page needs no other CDN.
- Model size grows with the square root of MW, kept between `min_pixels` and `max_pixels` on screen.
- **Mouse:** drag to move; hold the wheel (middle button) and drag to turn and tilt. The classic map has a "3D" button that opens this page.

## Data centres (database)

`data/datacenters.json` lists Romania's data centres, one record per site: operating buildings, companies present inside
another operator's building, and projects (under construction, permitted, announced, proposed). It is built by
`scripts/build_datacenters.py` from the files in `data/source/datacenters/`:

- `sites.csv` – the curated list, edited by hand (status, power, area, notes, sources);
- `source_matches.csv` – every row of datacenters.com, Inflect, Datacenter Atlas and DC Hub, and the site it was matched to;
- `peeringdb/` – a PeeringDB snapshot (operator-entered locations, networks, exchanges, carriers);
  refresh with `python3 scripts/build_datacenters.py --refresh-peeringdb`;
- `geocode_cache.json` – OpenStreetMap Nominatim results for sites without PeeringDB coordinates.

Each record has a reliability grade (A: the operator plus an independent source; B: the operator alone or two
independent sources; C: one source) and a location precision (building, address, street, town). Power figures are
entered only when a source states them clearly; conflicting figures are described in the notes instead.
