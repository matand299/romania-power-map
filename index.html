Sources:

  • ANRE licensed capacity register, 24.04.2023 (licensed plants)
  • ENTSO-E Transparency Platform, installed capacity per production unit (units of 100 MW and more, current capacity and later years)
  • Transelectrica (DEN), installed capacity: every storage installation in the grid, and the solar plants of its per-plant list that the other sources do not hold

Transelectrica plants are placed at the locality their name gives. A name shared by several localities is settled by the owner's or the name's county in the other sources; where that fails the plant counts in the totals but is not drawn. A storage unit at a plant already on the map sits at that plant.

This is an interim map. Each large unit reported by ENTSO-E replaces the register's rows for the same plant, so no plant appears twice; units built after the register's date are added, and capacity ENTSO-E lists only for later years is shown as under construction. Other plants commissioned after 24.04.2023 will be added only from verified sources, checked against the register in the same way.

*Licence lapsed: by the expiry date in the 2023 register the licence has since lapsed. The register predates any renewal, so the plant may hold a renewed licence and still operate. These plants are shown with a dashed outline.

Known gap: the register lists some plants only in part. ENTSO-E corrects this for units of 100 MW and more; smaller plants show the register's figures, and plants commissioned after 24.04.2023 below 100 MW are not yet on the map.

Cleaning: the register's subtotal rows removed.

Locations come from the source file and often mark the locality or county rather than the exact site.

Sites

What is here: Romania's data centres, one record per site: buildings in operation, projects (under construction, permitted, announced or proposed), companies with space inside another operator's building, and companies listed with a data centre whose building is unknown.

Sources: PeeringDB (sites entered by their operators, with exact locations, networks and internet exchanges), operators' own websites, datacenters.com, Inflect, Datacenter Atlas, DC Hub, Baxtel and press reports. Every row of every directory was matched to one site, so nothing appears twice.

Reliability: only independent confirmations count. The Atlas, DC Hub and Inflect copy much of PeeringDB, so they confirm nothing PeeringDB does not already say.

Locations: exact for sites in PeeringDB; otherwise from the address (street or town level, shown with a dashed halo). Several sites known only by town are spread around the town centre.

Power: entered only where a source states it. Where sources disagree the figure is left out and the conflict is described in the site's notes.
